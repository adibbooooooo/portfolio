Growth Roadmap
Growth that lasts is built on what you are already doing well, paired with deliberate edits
to the patterns that hold you back. The Continue / Stop / Try playbook below points to the
highest-leverage moves you can make from where you are today.

✓
CONTINUE

• Making fast, confident decisions with incomplete information; it works
• Holding people accountable and pushing past mediocrity
• Bringing creative solutions to complex, broken problems
• Speaking hard truths directly instead of hedging
• Building teams and environments with clear ownership

• Dismissing emotional or relational concerns as irrelevant obstacles
• Moving faster than stakeholders can process without checking in
• Assuming people have the same risk tolerance and decisiveness you
do

STOP

• Neglecting to explain the why behind your decisions and sense of
urgency
• Viewing consensus-building as weakness instead of smart stakeholder
navigation

!
TRY

• Pausing to ask how your directness lands before moving forward
• Staying curious about why people resist instead of pushing harder
• Documenting your reasoning so others can follow your logic
• Building explicit check-ins with people who process differently
• Experimenting with collaborative framing of decisions you lead

One Thing to Remember
If you only walked away from this guide with one thing, this would be it. The single
most leveraged insight from your profile, distilled into a sentence you can hold onto and a

★

Your impact multiplies when you bring people with
you, not just drag them forward.
You're right that decisive action beats endless deliberation, and your ability to see
what needs doing is rare. But the resistance you sometimes encounter isn't always
incompetence; it's often people who need to understand the why and see you value their
perspective. You don't need to sacrifice speed or conviction. You need to get skilled
at moving people in your direction so they run faster, not just follow harder. That's the
leverage that turns you from a brilliant solo operator into an unstoppable force.
